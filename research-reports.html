My interests connect geography, environmental change, and the practical use of spatial data.

Research interests

I am interested in how geospatial methods can help explain environmental patterns and their changes through time. Areas I have explored include historical imagery, vegetation mapping, Arctic shipping, and sea ice.

Across these topics, I am particularly interested in data quality, the choice of analytical methods, and how uncertainty affects the conclusions we draw.
