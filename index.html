About me

I'm Joshua, a master's student in Geography at the University of Ottawa. My interests include environmental mapping, remote sensing, and using spatial data to understand landscape change.

My background includes federal government co-op experience in GIS and data management. Through my academic projects, I work with historical imagery, satellite data, field observations, and Python to investigate geographic questions.

This portfolio brings together selected examples of my GIS work, remote sensing projects, and research.
