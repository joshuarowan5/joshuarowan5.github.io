Academic project · Mont Jacques-Cartier

Bringing historical imagery into a modern GIS

ArcGIS Pro · Ground control points · Image alignment

Historical aerial photographs provide a useful record of earlier landscapes. Before comparing them with recent imagery, the photographs need to be aligned with geographic reference data.

For my Mont Jacques-Cartier project, I identified control points and compared georeferencing transformations to improve alignment across the study area. This preparation supported the subsequent vegetation classification and change analysis.

Methods demonstrated

  • Identifying corresponding features in historical and recent imagery.
  • Evaluating how transformation choice affects spatial alignment.
  • Considering positional uncertainty when interpreting landscape change.
