01 · Academic project · Québec

Vegetation change at Mont Jacques-Cartier

ArcGIS Pro · Historical aerial photography · Landsat · Field observations

Question

How can historical and recent imagery help characterize vegetation patterns and change in the alpine environment of Mont Jacques-Cartier?

Data & approach

The project brings together historical aerial photographs, recent imagery, Landsat-derived NDVI, and field observations. Historical imagery was georeferenced before vegetation mapping and comparison.

The classification distinguishes dense vegetation, sparse woody vegetation or krummholz, and open alpine surfaces. NDVI and field measurements provide additional information for interpreting these patterns.

Skills demonstrated

  • Georeferencing imagery from different time periods.
  • Preparing training samples and classifying vegetation.
  • Calculating and summarizing vegetation indices.
  • Connecting field observations with remotely sensed patterns.

Interpreting change

Differences in image resolution, acquisition date, alignment, and class definitions affect comparisons. These factors need to be considered alongside any measured changes.

02 · Academic project · Python

Water mapping with satellite imagery

Multispectral imagery · Spectral indices · Machine learning

Question

How well do spectral indices and machine learning methods separate water from other surfaces in satellite imagery?

Data & approach

This project uses labelled multispectral image patches to compare water-mapping methods. The workflow includes a spectral-index baseline, machine learning models, and evaluation of predicted water masks against reference masks.

Skills demonstrated

  • Preparing image features and training data in Python.
  • Comparing baseline and machine learning methods.
  • Evaluating predictions with intersection over union and Dice scores.
  • Examining difficult scenes to understand model limitations.

Interpreting performance

Cloud shadows, sediment, narrow waterways, and mixed shoreline pixels can complicate water mapping. Scene-level evaluation helps reveal variation that an overall average can hide.
